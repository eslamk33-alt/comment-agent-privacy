Privacy Policy

Comment Agent

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Comment Agent collects, processes, uses, and protects information when managing comments on connected Facebook Pages.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, Comment Agent may process:

  • Facebook comment text and comment identifiers.
  • Public profile names and identifiers associated with comments.
  • Facebook post identifiers and relevant post content.
  • Suggested replies, approved replies, and publishing records.
  • Technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

  • Retrieve and organize comments on connected Facebook Pages.
  • Generate suggested replies using artificial intelligence.
  • Allow administrators to review replies before publication.
  • Publish approved replies on behalf of the connected Page.
  • Maintain operational records and prevent duplicate responses.

3. Artificial Intelligence Services

Comment text and relevant context may be sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate suggested responses. This processing is performed to provide the application's automated response features.

Information is shared with this service only as needed for the enabled functionality and subject to the applicable provider terms and privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Comment Agent may interact with the following services:

  • Meta Platforms: Facebook Graph API and Facebook Webhooks.
  • Google: Gemini AI for suggested response generation.
  • WhatsApp or Telegram: Only if optional notification or review integrations are enabled.

These services process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

Application data may be stored on the server hosting Comment Agent, including comment records, pending replies, publishing history, and operational logs.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for the period necessary to operate the application, maintain relevant records, and fulfill applicable legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of data and the application's configuration.

7. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be processed by Meta, Google, and optional notification providers when required to operate the features described in this policy.

8. User Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their interactions with Comment Agent by contacting the application operator using the email address below.

Requests will be reviewed and processed as applicable, subject to legal and operational requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or data processing practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.
